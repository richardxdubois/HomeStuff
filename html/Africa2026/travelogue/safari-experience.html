[DRAFT] This page is about what it actually felt like, day to day — the bush camps, the guides, the Land Cruisers and the dust, up before six and out by 06:30, the midday siesta, tea, back out in the late afternoon. Ninety degrees in Botswana; much cooler everywhere else.

[DRAFT] There's a progression worth capturing: discovery at the start, overwhelmed a little by how much wildlife there was and how many kinds; by Botswana, the same animals were familiar, so the interest shifted to the odd, watery situations they turned up in. The balloon over the Mara. The slick, informal bush-plane transfers — Cessnas, AirVans. And a quiet worry about over-tourism in the busiest spots, which is worth a paragraph of its own, gently.

[DRAFT] Fewer photos on this page than the others, on purpose — this is the page that's mostly words, with just enough images to place you there.
